# KharchaLens

> **Understand your spending. Protect your privacy.**

KharchaLens is a privacy-first, offline application that helps people understand where their money goes by analyzing bank statements.

## Why KharchaLens?

Many finance applications focus on budgeting or investments.

KharchaLens focuses on one simple question:

> **Where did my money go?**

## Principles

- 🔒 Privacy First
- 💻 Offline First
- 📊 Explain Before Visualizing
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Designed for Everyone
